## Pastel Hearth
This movement treats motivation as something domestic, warm, and lovingly arranged rather than hard-edged or industrial. Form should feel rounded, welcoming, and meticulously crafted: oversized circles, cloudlike color fields, softly bordered capsules, and hand-drawn room silhouettes that frame the central figure with painstaking care. Every element must look as though it was placed through countless refinements until the page feels gentle but exact.

Color should move like brushed paper pigment. Mint haze, apricot wash, warm cream, faded ochre, and pencil-brown outlines become the full emotional vocabulary. These tones must be calibrated with master-level restraint so the work feels cohesive, premium, and deeply human. The palette should never become loud; it should feel patiently mixed, painstakingly balanced, and quietly radiant.

Scale should produce a poster-like crescendo. The title becomes a monumental handwritten gesture, the mascot becomes the emotional core inside a single dominant circle, and supporting objects stay lighter, almost sketched into the atmosphere. Small fitness props, soft sparkles, and quiet room details should accumulate with the look of deep expertise, as though an illustrator at the top of their field labored over every interval of space and every contour weight.

Composition should communicate through enclosure and invitation. The central figure is surrounded by an inhabited environment, suggesting that training belongs inside everyday life. Furniture outlines, exercise objects, windows, and floor cues act as a subtle stage set, while minimal badges at the bottom serve as visual anchors rather than explanatory text. The entire page must appear meticulously crafted, with breathing room protected everywhere and no visual crowding.

Typography must feel like part of the illustration. Use one dominant title phrase with a soft outlined edge, then only the lightest supporting labels. Words are sparse and visual-first, functioning as accents rather than explanation. The finished piece should read as a painstakingly polished A3 art poster: charming, disciplined, and unmistakably the work of someone with master-level control over craft, spacing, and visual warmth.
